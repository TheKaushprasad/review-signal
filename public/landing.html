Powerful Capabilities

Everything you need to turn customer feedback into product decisions

Built for product managers who want instant clarity without manual tagging.

Jev Classification

Powered by Jev, TypeSafe AI's typed-decision model. A built-in offline classifier is also available, so you can run with no API key at all.

Sentiment & NPS Impact

Tag every review positive, neutral, or negative, with a 1–5 confidence score and a 1–5 NPS-impact rating so you can see what most threatens loyalty.

Smart Category Grouping

Automatically buckets each review into UI/UX, Employee, App bug, Payment, Order/Service, or Other — then filter the dashboard by any of them.

Two-Way Sheet Sync

Edit a sentiment or category inside the dashboard and the correction writes straight back to the matching row in your Google Sheet.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What classifier does Review Signal use?

By default it uses Jev by TypeSafe AI, a typed-decision model built for classification. There's also a built-in offline heuristic classifier you can switch to — it needs no API key and keeps everything on your own machine.

What does two-way sheet sync mean?

When you edit an AI-assigned category or sentiment in the dashboard, Review Signal updates the matching row in your connected Google Sheet — so your spreadsheet stays the source of truth.

Is my review data private?

With the Jev engine, review text is sent to TypeSafe AI for classification. If you need everything to stay local, switch to the offline classifier — no review text leaves your environment.

Can I try it without my own CSV?

Yes — open the app and click "Try the sample dataset" to load 20 example reviews and explore the full dashboard.
